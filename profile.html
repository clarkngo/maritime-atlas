About Maritime Atlas

Maritime Atlas is a browsable visual reference gallery — not a course. It collects diagrams and illustrations organised by maritime topic, drawing from both companion curricula: Maritime Operations & Industry Fundamentals (MOF-101) and Maritime Operational Technology Security (MOT-101). There are no lessons, objectives, quizzes, or simulations here — only visual reference material with source links back to the relevant lesson pages.

Companion Courses

MOF-101 — Maritime Operations & Industry Fundamentals

12-lesson prerequisite primer on ships, ports, navigation, cargo, regulation, and commercial shipping.

MOT-101 — Maritime Operational Technology Security

12-lesson continuation on securing shipboard and port operational technology for learners who complete MOF-101.

Scope & Intent

Every visual uses synthesised scenarios — none of it relies on real vessel, port, or company data. Schematics and illustrations are tagged separately; see Safeguards & Attribution and the Illustrations & Photos gallery.
