Safeguards & Attribution Policy

Maritime Atlas is a visual reference companion to the MOF-101 and MOT-101 curricula. Every diagram in this gallery follows the same safeguards as the parent courses.

No Real Operational Data

No visual uses real vessel names, IMO numbers, company logos, port facility layouts, network IP ranges, or any other identifiable operational data. All content is synthesised for educational use.

Schematics (SVG)

Tagged Schematic on gallery cards. Author-created educational diagrams — simplified illustrations, not technical drawings, survey data, or facility plans. Built as SVG files in /svg/.

Illustrations & Photos (Raster)

Tagged AI illustration or Photo. Raster files in /images/ must be human-reviewed before publication. AI-generated images require the generation tool and model in the attribution field. Licensed photos require source credit.

Same Categories, Different Tags

Illustrations are not siloed in a separate taxonomy — they sit alongside schematics in the same 12 topic categories, with visual-type badges and filters on the hub. The Illustrations & Photos page is a cross-category view of raster visuals only.

Educational Use Only

These visuals support classroom and self-study reference. They are not operational guidance, navigational aids, or security assessments for any real vessel or facility.

Course Cross-Links

Source tags on each card link back to the relevant MOF-101 or MOT-101 lesson page(s). Maritime Atlas supplements those lessons — it does not replace them.

Parent course safeguards

For the full responsible-use policies of each companion course, see the safeguards pages on MOF-101 and MOT-101.
